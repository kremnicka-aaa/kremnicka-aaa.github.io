President Hunt · QR cards

Teacher's notes

45 cards – one for every President (Grover Cleveland and Donald Trump served two non-consecutive terms, so the 47 presidencies have 45 cards). Print on A4, cut along the dashed lines and hide the cards around the classroom, the corridor or the school yard.

  1. Students open the camera on their phone and point it at a QR code. The link opens President Hunt (kremnicka-aaa.github.io/aaa5/hunt/) with that President.
  2. To get the card they answer a question (who? when? which party?) and take a sharp photo.
  3. Albums are saved only on each student's phone. No sign-in, no data collected.
  4. Ideas: a 15-minute hunt at the start of Unit 3 (the political system) or Unit 1 (the USA after 1945); groups compete for the most cards; each group presents three of “their” Presidents.

The card does not show the name, so students can't choose. The small number in the corner is for you (order of presidency).
